## Localization

Two languages supported: **Arabic (ar)** and **English (en)**, fully localized as of commit `f2fd7fc` (2026-05-08). Strings are in `lib/l10n/*.arb`, generated into `lib/generated/intl/messages_{ar,en}.dart` and accessed via `S.of(context)` from `lib/generated/l10n.dart`.

Locale is rebuilt on the fly via `MaterialApp(key: ValueKey(currentLang))` in `main.dart`.
